# Task 53: Multi-Seed Evaluation of Nano on Brev A100 Clips (Seeds 1–4)

## Overview
This directory contains the multi-seed evaluation of 19 clips generated by `raj-3` on the Brev A100 GPU (`http://100.111.167.68:8014`, diffusers pinned to `86ef420b`, 832x480, 189 frames @ 24 fps, steps=35, cfg=6.0, shift=10.0, no negative prompt).

Clips evaluated:
- **8 Mid-fall clips:** `box_mid_b1_noneg_s1..s4.mp4` and `box_mid_b2_noneg_s1..s4.mp4` (Ball 1 and Ball 2, seeds 1–4)
- **8 Held-ball control clips:** `box_ctrl_b1_noneg_s1..s4.mp4` and `box_ctrl_b2_noneg_s1..s4.mp4` (Ball 1 and Ball 2, seeds 1–4)
- **3 Pendulum clips:** `box_pend1_noneg_s2.mp4` and `box_pendmid_dd50_noneg_s1..s2.mp4`

## Key Findings

### 1. Zero Energy Dissipation / Perpetual Bouncing is Seed-Invariant ($e_T \approx 1.0$)
- Across all 8 mid-fall clips ($n=8$, seeds 1–4, 2 scenes), mean log-linear restitution is:
  $$\mathbf{e_T = 0.9886 \pm 0.0110 \text{ (mean} \pm \text{SE)}}, \quad [\min 0.9368, \max 1.0298]$$
- **100% (8/8) of clips are still bouncing at frame 189** ($7.9$ s), recording $25.1 \pm 1.9$ contacts per clip (range $19 - 33$).
- In contrast, real GAUGE mocap exhibits $e_T \approx 0.693$ and comes to complete rest after only 4 bounces in $1.40$ s.
- **Tether hallucination artifact:** Seed 2 has extreme vertical strap brightness excess ($+90.6$ on Ball 1, $+109.5$ on Ball 2) indicating a hallucinated tether, while Seed 3 exhibits untethered perpetual bouncing ($+13.9$ and $+5.4$ excess, $e_T = 0.9931$ and $0.9994$).

### 2. Held-Ball Release Failure is Seed-Dependent
- **Seed 3:** Fails to release held ball from the gripper; spawns a duplicate/phantom ball in the lower corridor (`phantom_duplicate_unreleased`, gripper diff $< 44$, lower diff $> 34$).
- **Seed 2:** Genuinely opens gripper and releases ball at frame 27 (`genuine_release`, gripper diff $> 172$).
- **Seeds 1 and 4:** Exhibit delayed release at frame 94–98 (seed 1) or frame 13 (seed 4).

### 3. Pendulum Period
- Frame 0 release (seed 2): period $T = 2.65$ s (measured by `pend_acf`) / $1.28$ s (`measure_pend`).
- Mid-motion (seeds 1 & 2): $T = 3.77$ s and $5.56$ s.
- In all cases, substantially slower than real mocap ($T = 1.05$ s).
