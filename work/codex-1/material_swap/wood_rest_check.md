# Wood seed 2: descriptive pixel stability check

For the original `t97_surface_wood_s2.mp4` (SHA-256 `039d1fc1dada8cbe9101d70ca4a917ef2e179e3404c07ad7b3fd804ff5a3fb67`), `python3 check_rest.py out/t97_surface_wood_s2.mp4` decodes 189 frames and selects the largest connected grayscale <90 region inside fixed ROI x=[360,470), y=[210,310). This mask covers much of the visually identified ball at frame 100; selection and the window were chosen after viewing the clip. During frames 40–188 inclusive the mask centroid spans 3.940 px horizontally and 2.148 px vertically, and its maximum Euclidean displacement from frame 40 is 4.479 px. Mask area varies from 4,919 to 5,217 pixels. The first and last centroids are (416.531,266.155) and (412.922,268.159).

This supports the earlier qualitative description of little late screen-space movement, but is not a physical rest measurement: appearance, illumination, shape, camera movement, threshold choice and partial occlusion can shift the centroid; there is no physical scale or uncertainty calibration. It does not estimate restitution, generalize to wood, compare surfaces, or pass the planted cases. This is one selected T2V clip, not a matched trial. The initial drop begins off-screen and the clip is ineligible for a decay ratio as described in `two_clip_qc.md`.

## Threshold sensitivity, shift 56

Using the same fixed ROI and frames 40–188, `rest_threshold_sensitivity.py` varies the grayscale cutoff after the original visual inspection; this is descriptive, not a preregistered validation. At cutoffs 60, 75, 90, 105 and 120, maximum centroid displacement from frame 40 is respectively 4.37, 4.55, 4.48, 3.74 and 3.79 pixels (149 frames each). The component areas change from 3,231–3,584 to 5,905–6,140 pixels across the extreme thresholds; centroid stability does not establish correct segmentation, physical rest, restitution or material dependence.
