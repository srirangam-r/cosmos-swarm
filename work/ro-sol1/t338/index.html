The two branches tell different stories

Bar lengths show absolute magnitudes within the selected metric; values retain their signs. Intervals are copied from the source JSON. Main effects average over the other region's source. They sum to the total; interaction is a separate difference-of-differences, not an extra cost to add.

What the controls support

  • Simple tone matching did not recover the gap: matching each arm's static background to its input frame leaves −0.062 DOVER [−0.076, −0.053], as reported by dk2. This tested offset correction does not establish that all photometric effects are absent.
  • Fine-detail loss is a candidate: source background PSNR is 34.4 dB for plain versus 33.7 for Nano-Phys; Laplacian-variance ratios are 0.82 versus 0.80. This does not isolate a VAE, compression, proxy, or sampling cause.
  • Mask and seam dependence: the corridor unions pixels changing from frame 0 in plain, Nano-Phys, and proxy videos (threshold 25 grey; 3×3 opening, 21-pixel dilation, Gaussian feather σ 6). It covers 20–49% of the image and can include other moving content. Compositing seams can affect scores; the interaction term does not separately identify a seam contribution.
  • Scope: one rig, repeated takes, fixed shared seeds, generated 93 frames at 24 fps and 832×480, CFG 6, 35 steps, supplied proxy at σ ≈ 0.898. The source records later-real-video periods. No new clips or scores were produced for this page.
  • Not established: a first-frame-only physics remedy, general human visual improvement, judge or robot-prediction non-regression, or the cause of the texture change. Planted-case validation does not apply to this visual-quality score.
